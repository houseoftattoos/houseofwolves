Wait times vary by artist and the scale of the piece, typically ranging from a few weeks to several months for larger custom work. Your artist will give you an accurate estimate during your consultation.

Pricing depends on size, placement, detail and the artist's hourly rate. You'll receive a personalised quote following your consultation, once the design and scope are finalised.

Yes, we take a deposit to secure your appointment, and your artist will go over the details when you book. Deposits are non-refundable but can be transferred to a new booking with at least 48 hours' notice.

Smaller pieces can often be completed in a single sitting. Larger works, such as sleeves or body suits, are typically broken into multiple sessions scheduled over time.

We ask for at least 48 hours' notice to reschedule or cancel an appointment. Deposits are non-refundable but can be transferred to a new booking within this window.

Arrive well-rested and hydrated, eat beforehand, and avoid alcohol for 24 hours prior. Wear comfortable clothing that allows easy access to the area being tattooed.

You need to be 18 or over to get tattooed with us, in line with Australian law.

Your artist will talk you through aftercare at the end of your appointment. In general, keep the tattoo clean, moisturise lightly, avoid soaking it in baths, pools or the ocean, keep it out of direct sun, and don't pick or scratch while it heals. If anything doesn't seem right, get in touch with your artist.
