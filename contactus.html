Bookings & Consultations

House of Wolves is an appointment-only studio based in Geelong, Australia. We take on a limited number of custom projects each year to ensure every client receives the time, attention and consideration their project deserves.

To begin the process, please get in touch with the artist you'd like to work with via Instagram. All consultations and bookings are arranged directly through their DM or email.

Inbox us with:

  • A brief description of your idea
  • Placement on the body
  • Reference images (if any)
  • Any other details
