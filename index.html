About

House of Wolves

We are a private, appointment only studio in Geelong West specialising in Japanese Irezumi, American & Neo-Traditional and Fine Line tattooing. Every piece across the studio — from large scale full body suits and sleeve work to smaller fine line commissions — is custom designed one on one between artist and client.

Our resident artists bring their own distinctive approach to their craft, creating everything from bold traditional and illustrative work to intricate ornamental pieces. We welcome clients from Geelong, the Bellarine Peninsula and the Surf Coast.

Booking & Consultations

All bookings go directly through the artist you'd like to work with, via Instagram DM — include your idea or reference imagery, placement and approximate size. We don't offer over the phone consultations. Wait times vary by artist and scale of the piece, from a few weeks up to several months for larger custom work.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept walk-ins?

We're predominantly an appointment-based studio. While we occasionally accommodate walk ins depending on artist availability, we recommend booking a consultation in advance to secure your preferred date and artist.

How do I book a consultation?

Reach out to your preferred artist directly via Instagram with a brief description of your idea, placement and size. They'll arrange a consultation to discuss the design in detail.

How much do tattoos cost?

Pricing depends on size, placement, detail and the artist's hourly rate. You'll receive a personalised quote following your consultation, once the design and scope are finalised.

Do you tattoo over existing work?

Yes, several of our artists specialise in cover ups and reworking existing tattoos. Send through clear photos of the piece you'd like addressed and we'll advise on the best approach.
